## Description

```
Neural networks, the models behind much of the artificial intelligence in the news, built up from a single neuron: what they do, how to stop training them, and when they pay off.

This is video 9 of 10 in Machine Learning Basics for Marine Science, a free introductory course for marine researchers. This video goes with section 9 · Introduction to neural networks.

In this video:
1. Build curves from simple bends: each neuron is a weighted sum with a bend, and the bends add up to curves.
2. Stop training at the right step: watch the validation error, and keep the weights from its lowest point.
3. Let a network find combinations: it pays off when the answer depends on a combination of inputs that nobody can name.
```
